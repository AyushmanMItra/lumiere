01 • THE PROBLEM

Watching your own movie with friends is fundamentally broken.

Watching high-quality, local movie files (like 2 GB+ .mkv or .mp4 rips) remotely with friends is fundamentally broken on the modern web. Traditional streaming architectures force you to upload massive files to a cloud server — which takes hours and incurs heavy server costs.

Attempting to stream those heavy files directly browser-to-browser (P2P) overheats mobile devices, drains batteries within an hour, and inevitably crashes Google Chrome due to strict WebAssembly memory limits.

Furthermore, modern browsers natively reject complex video containers like .mkv, making standard web-based playback nearly impossible without heavy, CPU-melting transcoding.

02 • CURRENT OPTIONS

And every existing tool asks you to compromise.

COMPROMISE

Walled Gardens

TELEPARTY, AMAZON WATCH PARTY, ETC.

You are strictly limited to whatever is currently licensed on Netflix, Prime, Hulu. Your own .mkv rip, your indie short film, your school project — none of it is welcome inside those parties.

COMPROMISE

Screen Sharing

DISCORD, ZOOM, GOOGLE MEET

The host must record their screen and live-stream it. That means massive upload bandwidth, compression blur, dropped frames, choppy 15fps playback, and audio that drifts a second behind by the third act.

COMPROMISE

Cloud-Sync Sites

UPLOAD-THEN-WATCH SERVICES

Everyone waits hours while the host uploads a 3GB file to a third-party server, hits upload limits, and hands their private movie to some anonymous cloud. The party is dead before it starts.

03 • OUR SOLUTION

Lumière makes the host's browser the projector.

The movie never has to move. It stays on the host's own machine, plays through their own hardware decoder (which happily eats .mkv, 10-bit HEVC, and every weird codec their OS supports), and only one thing is broadcast to everyone else: the current playhead position and — when the host chooses — a live stream of the frame itself over WebRTC and memory remuxing.

Meanwhile, voice, camera, and text chat run peer-to-peer alongside it in the same room, so a movie night ends up feeling like everyone is on the same couch, without ever asking anyone to wait for an upload bar to finish.

04 • WHY WE'RE DIFFERENT

Built for friends, not subscriptions.

Lumière is 100% free, private, self-hostable, and open source. There are no logins, no paywalls, no bandwidth meters, and no third-party tracking. Just you, your movie, and your friends.
